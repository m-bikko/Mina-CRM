## Product Overview
**One-liner:** Minawear is a women's clothing boutique in Kazakhstan with ordering via WhatsApp.
**What it does:**
- An online storefront (catalogue with photos, sizes and prices; cart). The order is sent as a WhatsApp message to the shop, and the owner fulfils it manually.
- **Online-only** (no showroom). Delivery: courier within the shop's city plus post across all of Kazakhstan (confirmed by the owner). The city name is not specified, so do not name a city in SEO copy.
**Product category:** женская одежда / интернет-магазин женской одежды (Kazakhstan). How customers search: «женская одежда купить», «атласная рубашка женская», «кардиган женский», «джинсы baggy женские», «мини юбка», «брюки из драпа», «спортивные штаны флис», plus «… Казахстан», «… с доставкой по Казахстану». No city keywords: online-only, city not confirmed.
**Product type:** e-commerce (small boutique, a single owner).
**Business model:** direct sales of clothing, priced 5 500–15 990 ₸. Real discounts exist on many items (e.g. cardigans 12 990 → 8 990 ₸).

## Target Audience
**Target customers:** women ~18–35 across Kazakhstan who buy clothes from Instagram/TikTok boutiques. The site is trilingual (ru default, kk, en) as confirmed by the owner, to capture Kazakh-language queries.
**Primary use case:** find stylish, affordable everyday clothes (satin shirts, knit, denim, comfy fleece) and order quickly via WhatsApp without marketplace hassle.
**Jobs to be done:**
- «Собрать образ на каждый день и на вечер без похода по ТРЦ»
- «Купить понравившуюся вещь из Instagram быстро — написать и получить»
- «Найти вещь по фото, в нужном размере, по понятной цене»

## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** marketplaces such as Kaspi, Wildberries, Lamoda and Ozon are overloaded and impersonal. Instagram shops answer slowly and don't show prices.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- marketplaces: thousands of identical listings, hard to trust quality and fit;
- Instagram boutiques: «цена в директ», no catalogue, slow answers.
**Emotional tension:** fear the item won't fit or will look different than in the photo.
